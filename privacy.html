Data practices

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 October 2026. This policy applies to the OwnVenture Analytics local connector and this informational website.

Operator and purpose

The OwnVenture channel owner operates the connector to analyse the channel's videos and improve content quality and consistency. Google access is authorised by the owner.

Data accessed

The connector accesses channel identity, channel statistics and video information, and aggregate performance reports such as views, watch time, engagement and subscriber changes. Reports requested for the owner's analysis can also include retention, traffic sources and audience breakdowns where YouTube makes them available. Public comments may be read when the owner requests community analysis.

Access uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API with the read-only scopes youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly. Google account passwords are not collected by the connector.

Use and AI-assisted analysis

Data is used for reporting, comparing performance and developing OwnVenture's content strategy. At the owner's direction, selected analytics may be supplied to OpenAI's Codex or ChatGPT to assist with that analysis. Such processing is subject to the provider's applicable terms and account data controls. The local connector itself does not train AI models, sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

OwnVenture Analytics' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

Retrieved reports and connector configuration are stored on the owner's Windows computer. OAuth tokens are encrypted with Windows user-specific credential protection; the private credential directory has restricted local access. Reports remain until the owner deletes them or replaces them with a later snapshot. The connector's current initial-report files are replaced on subsequent runs.

No channel analytics, OAuth credentials or tokens are included in this public website or its source repository. This static website has no analytics scripts, advertising scripts, contact forms or account-connection features.

Website hosting

GitHub Pages hosts the website. GitHub may process visitor network information, including IP addresses, for hosting and security under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Visiting this website does not authorise Google account access.

Access control, revocation and deletion

The owner can revoke the connector's Google access in Google Account connections. Revocation prevents further authorised access but does not automatically delete existing local reports. The owner can delete local reports and saved credentials separately. If selected data has been supplied to an AI service, the owner manages that conversation and its retention through that service's controls.

Contact and updates

For questions or deletion requests, contact the channel owner using the contact options on the OwnVenture YouTube channel. Material changes to these data practices will be reflected here before the changed use begins.

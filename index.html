OwnVenture Analytics

A local, read-only reporting tool used by the OwnVenture channel owner to understand video performance and improve future content.

This site explains the connector and its data practices. Channel analytics and Google credentials are stored separately on the owner's computer.

What the connector does

It retrieves channel and video information through the YouTube Data API and performance reports through the YouTube Analytics API. The owner uses these reports to compare videos, understand audience retention and engagement, and guide editorial decisions.

Why it requests Google access

Private channel analytics require the channel owner's permission. The connector requests youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly. It uses the owner's Google sign-in in a separate browser authorisation flow.

These permissions support reading channel information and analytics. The connector has no publishing, editing or deletion functions.

Who uses it

This is an owner-operated tool for OwnVenture. It is not a public account-connection service, and this website has no sign-in form.

Read the Privacy Policy for data access, storage, AI-assisted analysis and deletion details, and the Terms of Use for the tool's scope.
